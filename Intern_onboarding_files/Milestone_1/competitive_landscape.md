# Understand competitive landscape #56

## This document contains a brief competitive analysis of Focus Bear against other productivity and focus apps, including Freedom, Cold Turkey, Tiimo, and Routinery

**What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?**

Compared to Freedom and Cold Turkey, Focus Bear's biggest differentiator is its additional scheduling and task management capabilities — it's not just a blocker.
On the other hand, apps like Tiimo and Routinery focus purely on routine and schedule planning without any website or app blocking, so they don't directly help users stay focused during work.
Focus Bear combines both sides — blocking distractions while also guiding users through their day — and is purpose-built for people with ADHD rather than being a general productivity tool.

**If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?**

Because it combines schedule/task planning and distraction blocking into a single app.
Other apps only solve one of these problems, and as a user, I don't want to juggle multiple apps just to manage my day.

**What's one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn't?**

Cold Turkey can extend blocking to other devices such as phones and tablets.
Tiimo and Routinery both offer voice guidance (TTS) and AI-generated planning — where users describe what they need to do and the app automatically creates a schedule.
This is far more convenient than setting everything up manually.

**What's one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?**

I think Focus Bear could benefit from adding STT (Speech-to-Text) and TTS (Text-to-Speech) support.
During schedule setup, users could speak their tasks and goals, which would then be processed by an LLM to automatically generate a schedule
and estimate time for each activity.
This would make the planning process significantly faster and less overwhelming — especially for ADHD users who may find manual configuration time-consuming and off-putting.
