# My learning goals answer

* What do you hope to learn from this internship?

  * I also look forward to experiencing how a real development team divides tasks and collaborates, which I believe will help me strengthen my communication and teamwork skills.

* What skills are you most excited to develop?
  * **Cross-platform mobile development** — gaining hands-on experience building apps that work seamlessly across both iOS and Android.
  * **Real-world development workflows** — participating in code reviews, version control, and agile team practices.
  * **Team communication** — improving my ability to collaborate effectively with teammates across different roles and areas of expertise.
* What do you think you can contribute to the team?
  * **Mobile development** — I have some experience with iOS development, which will help me get up to speed quickly and contribute to the codebase early on.
  * **Backend knowledge** — I'm familiar with frameworks such as FastAPI, Spring Boot, and Flask, which gives me a solid understanding of how mobile apps communicate with backend services and APIs.
  * **Testing** — I have experience writing unit tests, which means I can help ensure code quality and reliability from the start.
* What is one area you feel less confident in that you’d like to improve?
  * **Cross-platform development** — My experience is mainly in iOS development, and I'm not yet familiar with cross-platform frameworks such as React Native or Flutter. I'm keen to learn and get hands-on practice with them.
  * **Android development** — I have limited exposure to the Android ecosystem and development environment, and I'd like to build more solid experience through real projects.
  * **Industry-level development workflows** — Most of my experience comes from personal or academic projects. I'm not yet familiar with the full cycle of professional feature development — from requirements gathering and design through to release — and I hope to gain that experience here.
* What does success in this internship look like for you?
  * **Being able to independently develop a small cross-platform application** — not just a backend API with a web frontend, but a real application that runs on desktop, iOS and Android.
  * **Getting familiar with industry-level development workflows** — experiencing the full cycle of a real project, from requirements and design through to release.
  * **Improving my communication and collaboration skills** — being able to work effectively within a team, understand different roles, and make a meaningful contribution.
