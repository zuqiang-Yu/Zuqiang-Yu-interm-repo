# Understanding of Focus Bear's mission and vision #58

**Why was Focus Bear created?**

Focus Bear was created because Jeremy Nagel and his team recognised that ADHD is a significant issue affecting people's daily lives, work, and studies — causing major economic losses and
negatively impacting the wellbeing of those affected. Jeremy himself is a late-diagnosed AuDHDer
who found that no existing productivity app worked for his brain, so he decided to build his own —
a tool genuinely designed for the neurodivergent community to help users improve their focus.

**What problem is Focus Bear solving?**

Focus Bear addresses the core challenges faced by people with ADHD in daily life:

- reducing distractions from other apps and websites (such as the impulse to scroll through social media),
- difficulty refocusing after being interrupted, disorganised routines, trouble maintaining a consistent schedule,
- weak time perception, and low motivation.
  Focus Bear tackles these systematically through website and app blocking, routine guidance, and structured focus sessions.

**Why is this mission important?**

Approximately 10% of the global population has ADHD, and diagnosis rates are rising rapidly.
ADHD also has a significant economic impact — studies estimate it costs the US economy
around $84 billion per year. Yet most productivity tools are designed entirely for neurotypical users
and are largely ineffective for people with ADHD. These individuals are not lazy or lacking
self-control — their brains simply work differently and require different tools.
Focus Bear fills a long-neglected gap in the market.

**How does Focus Bear's mission align with my personal values or interests?**

As a mobile app developer, I believe good software should solve real user problems
rather than simply stacking features. Focus Bear was built from lived experience and is
genuinely user-centred in its design. I'm also interested in AI and mobile technology,
and Focus Bear is combining both to support people with specific needs —
which makes this work feel meaningful to me.

**Do I personally relate to the challenges Focus Bear aims to solve?**

Yes, but can be better. As a student and developer, I've experienced the urge to pick up my phone,
struggled to maintain a consistent routine and day plan, and found it hard to refocus
after being interrupted. While I haven't been diagnosed with ADHD, these challenges
are very real for many people, and I can genuinely relate to what Focus Bear is trying to solve.
I also believe that with today's advances in AI, integrating AI to help users plan their day
and intelligently determine which apps and websites to block could make the app significantly
more effective.

In college, I didn't pay much attention to ADHD, but there were many friends or classmates around me who had difficulty concentrating and often got distracted by their mobile phones when doing assignments or reading papers. Some of them, however, I have found that they may feel shy or scared when they first come into contact with others or socialize in a public setting. But the warm inquiries and greetings from others usually make them feel comfortable and join in the social interaction.

I think focus bear can play an important role in helping them improve their concentration and plan their daily life during work and study.
