## Communication Channels

Use the right channel based on how urgent something is:

* **Discord** – for general questions and check-ins (I usually reply within a few hours during work hours)
  e.g. "Can you take a look at this when you get a chance?"

* **Email** – for detailed feedback, task briefs, or anything I need to refer back to
  e.g. "Here are the requirements for this week's task"

* **Call/SMS** – for true urgencies only
  e.g. "Something's broken in production and I need your help now"

## How I Work Best

* I do my best work when:
   * Tasks are broken down into clear, concrete steps
   * I know what "done" looks like before I start
   * I have time to focus without too many interruptions
   * I can ask a clarifying question upfront rather than guess and go in the wrong direction
   * I have a structured to-do list to work through

* I struggle when:
   * Requirements are vague or keep changing
   * I have to context-switch between many unrelated tasks at once
   * I receive verbal-only instructions for complex tasks with no written follow-up

## Communication Style

* I prefer messages to be:
   * **Direct** — no need to soften things; tell me clearly what needs to change
   * **Written** — a short written summary after a verbal conversation helps me make sure I understood correctly
   * **Specific** — vague feedback is hard to act on; pointing to the exact issue is much more useful

* The best way to reach me is:
   * Discord for day-to-day questions
   * Written comments in PRs or tasks for feedback on my work
   * A scheduled call or video meeting for anything complex or nuanced
