# Roles in an Agile Team

## Research & Learn

### Key Roles in an Agile Team

#### 1. Developers

Write, test, and implement new features by participating in sprint planning, daily stand-ups, and collaborating with QA to deliver working software each sprint.

#### 2. QA (Quality Assurance)

Ensure product quality by writing and running test cases, reporting bugs, and verifying developer fixes before each release.

#### 3. Product Manager (PM)

Define the product vision and prioritise the backlog by writing user stories, maintaining the roadmap, and communicating with stakeholders on feature scope.

#### 4. Agile Project Manager (Agile PM / Scrum Master)

Facilitate the Agile process by running sprint ceremonies, tracking velocity, and removing blockers so the team can stay focused.

#### 5. Other Supporting Roles

- **UX Designer:** Designs interfaces and improves user experience to guide development decisions.
- **Stakeholders:** Provide business requirements and feedback to shape product direction.
- **Customer Support:** Relay real user issues back to the team to inform bug fixes and feature priorities.

---

### How Roles Collaborate: Kanban vs. Scrum

In Scrum, the team works in time-boxed sprints (maybe 1–2 weeks) with defined roles — Scrum Master, Product Owner, and Dev Team — who meet regularly in ceremonies like stand-ups, sprint planning, and retrospectives to deliver a set of features each sprint.

In Kanban, there are no fixed roles or iterations; instead, work flows continuously and team members collaborate on-demand as tasks move across the board, with the focus on reducing bottlenecks rather than hitting sprint goals.

## Reflection

### What is my role, and how does it contribute to the Agile process?

My role is **Developer**. I contribute to the Agile process by turning user stories and requirements into working software within each sprint.
I will participate in sprint planning to estimate effort, attend daily stand-ups to flag blockers, and collaborate with QA to resolve bugs quickly.

---

### How do my responsibilities overlap or interact with other roles?

As a Developer, I work most closely with **QA** — I hand off completed features for testing and act on bug reports they raise.

I also interact with the **PM** to clarify the acceptance criteria on user stories. The **Agile PM** helps me by removing blockers (e.g., unclear requirements or dependencies on other teams) so I can stay focused on delivery.

There is some overlap with **UX** when implementing UI components, where I need to follow design specs closely and raise issues if something is technically unfeasible.

---

## Task

### How my role fits into the Agile team

My primary responsibility is to take prioritised user stories from the backlog and turn them into working, tested features by the end of each sprint. I collaborate with QA to ensure quality, with the PM to understand what value we're delivering, and with the Agile PM to stay unblocked and on track. Without developers building and shipping, the Agile cycle cannot produce tangible outcomes for users.

---

### One role I frequently collaborate with — and how we can improve teamwork

As a Developer, I frequently collaborate with **QA**. They test the features I build and report bugs for me to fix. To improve our teamwork, I could involve QA earlier — sharing my implementation plan before coding starts so they can prepare test cases in advance.
