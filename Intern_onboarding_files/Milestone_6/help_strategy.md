# When you get stuck - what next?

## When do you prefer using AI vs. searching Google?

For almost all problems, I prefer to use ai. When the answers given by ai fail to solve my problem, I will search for relevant video tutorials on the searching engine.

## How do you decide when to ask a colleague instead?

This depends on whether the issue is sensitive (data, technology, etc.) and whether I have already looked it up on ai and search engines. The most important point is whether this problem is urgent.

## What challenges do developers face when troubleshooting alone?

Debugging alone means there's no one to immediately point out blind spots, so developers often get stuck staring at the same code without noticing a simple mistake. Without a second pair of eyes, it's also easy to go down the wrong path for too long before realizing the root cause is somewhere else entirely.
